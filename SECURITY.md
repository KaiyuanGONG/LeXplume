# Security policy

## Report a vulnerability privately

If you believe you found a security or privacy vulnerability in LeXplume, do not open a public issue. Email [support@lexplume.com](mailto:support@lexplume.com) with the subject “Security report”.

Please include:

- the affected URL, app version and platform;
- a concise description of the impact;
- reproducible steps or a minimal proof of concept;
- whether any real user data may have been exposed; and
- a safe way to contact you.

Do not include API keys, passwords, access tokens, private vocabulary, account exports or another person's data. If sensitive evidence is necessary, first ask for a safe transfer method.

## Safe testing

Use only accounts and data you own or are expressly authorized to test. Do not disrupt service, exhaust quotas, access other users' information, use social engineering, or publish an unresolved vulnerability.

LeXplume does not currently operate a bug-bounty program. A report may be acknowledged and investigated, but no payment or public-credit commitment is implied.

## Scope

The supported surface is the current app served from [https://lexplume.com](https://lexplume.com) and any officially distributed LeXplume Android build. Copies, unofficial packages, third-party AI providers and user-configured endpoints are outside LeXplume's direct control, although a vulnerability in how LeXplume integrates them is still relevant.
