# Frequently asked questions

## Is LeXplume publicly available?

Not yet. LeXplume 1.0 is being prepared as a free, invite-only Beta for adults aged 18+. Public signup and payment are not enabled.

## Does it work offline?

After the Web app has loaded once, capture, your library, backup and review keep working offline. Network-dependent dictionary, AI, account and sync features need connectivity. Exact offline behavior can vary by browser and installed-app state.

## Where is my vocabulary stored?

LeXplume is local-first. The active copy is stored by your browser on the current Web origin. Clearing site data, changing origin or some browser cleanup settings can remove it, so export JSON backups regularly.

## Is cloud sync required?

No. Invited accounts can keep a copy in the cloud for sync, but the app always works from the copy on your device.

## Do I need an AI key?

No for the basic vocabulary and review workflow. For browser-direct AI, you provide a compatible key that is stored locally and excluded from export and sync. Invited accounts can use Cloud AI instead, which has a daily limit.

## Which languages can I study?

English is the default study language. French can be enabled separately. The interface language, explanation language and language of the word being studied are independent settings.

## Is there an Android app?

An Android Trusted Web Activity is being tested, but it is not a public Play release. Use only a listing linked from [https://lexplume.com](https://lexplume.com) when an official build becomes available.

## Is LeXplume intended for children or schools?

No. The current product and Beta are for adults aged 18+ learning on their own, and are not designed or marketed for children, schools or classroom administration.

## How do I delete my account?

Follow [https://lexplume.com/delete-account](https://lexplume.com/delete-account). Because local and cloud data are separate, the flow explains how to keep or erase the current device's local data.
