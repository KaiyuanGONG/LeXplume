# Product direction

This page describes direction, not a delivery promise or release calendar.

## Current focus

- Finish the free, invite-only 18+ Beta safety and operations gates.
- Preserve reliable local-first capture, JSON backup and FSRS review.
- Validate Web/PWA/Android storage continuity, offline behavior and accessibility on real devices.
- Complete privacy, account-deletion, support and generated-content reporting operations.
- Release Android only after Trusted Web Activity association and Play testing pass.

## Later, subject to evidence

- Gradually broaden account access if abuse, cost, support and privacy controls prove sustainable.
- Improve English/French enrichment and learning workflows from measured user feedback.
- Evaluate a paid Cloud AI plan only after separate legal, provider-permission, accounting, entitlement and billing decisions.

## Explicitly not promised

- A public launch date, country-by-country availability or permanent free Cloud AI quota.
- Mainland-China promotion or local-store distribution.
- School, classroom or child-directed features.
- Play Billing, in-app purchases or a native rewrite.

Current availability and policy links live at [https://lexplume.com](https://lexplume.com).
